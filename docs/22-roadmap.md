# 22. Roadmap

## Project Goal

Outline the release path for Harvest Tracker and the next steps after the initial implementation.

## Purpose

This document gives the team a realistic plan for rolling out the system and extending it over time.

## Business Context

The initial release covers core farm, harvest, worker, sales, dashboard, and report features. The next value will come from improving reliability, automation, and adaptability for future fruit support.

## Functional Requirements

- Release the initial version with core modules.
- Improve data quality and reporting.
- Expand into future features such as weather, disease detection, and market price prediction.

## Non Functional Requirements

- The roadmap should stay practical and achievable.
- The platform should remain modular as it grows.
- The upgrade path should avoid major rework.

## Business Rules

- Each milestone should be tied to a tangible business benefit.
- New features should be incorporated without breaking existing workflows.
- Multi-fruit support should be considered from the beginning.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The roadmap is clear for the team and future contributors.
- Each milestone has a practical timeline and objective.
- The expansion plan aligns with the architecture.
